Privacy policy — rclonec

This personal rclone configuration accesses the owner's Google Drive files and file metadata only to perform file operations requested by the owner.

Data is transferred between Google Drive and storage destinations selected by the owner. Stored copies remain until the owner deletes them.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's rclone configuration. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections.
